The installer deliberately does not add this self-signed certificate to the
Windows trust stores. Before trusting it manually, confirm that the certificate
subject is CN=Thrash, its v3.1.2 thumbprint is
7B479F26B3952BAC8E8F12FA2DE311B7783BA57B, and verify the published SHA-256 checksums against the
official GitHub release.
